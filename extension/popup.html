How to capture a session

  1. In a normal tab, sign in to the provider (e.g. chatgpt.com). Google and 2‑step verification work as usual.
  2. Open the connect link your bot or terminal gave you — it looks like …/connect/<token>.
  3. On that page, click Capture session. This extension reads the signed‑in session and sends it to the server in that link.
